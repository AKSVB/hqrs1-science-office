Role: Competitive Analyst. Filed 2026-09-27.

Method and caveats. All figures come from web search result snippets retrieved on 2026-09-27. Direct fetches of instagram.com, HypeAuditor, Modash, Heepsy, Social Blade, Instastatistics and most press sites were blocked, so per-post saves/shares could not be pulled. Follower counts are (a) the Instagram profile-page snippet, (b) a third-party tracker snippet with its date, or (c) a press article. "Observed (unverified)" marks descriptions with no URL. The brief's suggested @drjoe could not be found (closest verified: @drjoehanson); @wonderofscience on Instagram is tiny, which is itself a finding.

## 2. Fast-growing accounts of 2025–2026 and their playbooks

NASA and @nasaartemis (event tentpole). +4.6M on @nasa and +2M (+66%) on @nasaartemis during the April 2026 Artemis II mission; 149.4M views of launch/flyby/splashdown on NASA platforms, 100M+ engagements across TikTok and Instagram in mission week (https://www.nasa.gov/general/nasas-artemis-ii-breaks-agency-streaming-record/ ; https://www.fastcompany.com/91525305/nasa-moon-social-media-analytics ; https://almcorp.com/blog/artemis-ii-social-media-stats/). Lesson: tentpole calendars (launches, eclipses, Nobel week, Perseids) are the cheapest predictable reach; the surge goes to whoever explains the event fastest and clearest.

Zack D Films (repeatable animated formula). From a 2021 channel to 28.4M YouTube subscribers and ~10.6–11M on Instagram by mid-2026. Structure: strange opening question, visual reveal, fast explanation, final twist (https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/WebAnimation/ZackDFilms ; https://www.autoclips.app/zack-d-films-style-3d-videos). Unofficial Hindi and Farsi dub accounts exist (https://www.instagram.com/zackdfilms.hindi/ ; https://www.instagram.com/zackdfilms.farsi/): demand in non-English markets the original does not serve.

Cleo Abram (long-form authority, short-form distribution). One produced episode a month on a several-hundred-hour cycle (https://wpcreator.washingtonpost.com/p/creator-q-a-cleo-abram); ideation and hooks discussed with Colin and Samir (https://podcasts.apple.com/ph/podcast/how-cleo-abram-dominated-youtube-in-1-year/id1379942034?i=1000669821146). One deep piece feeds many vertical cuts; access is the differentiator.

Astro Alexandra (credibility plus daily cadence). Instagram described as "her polished extension of her TikTok content" (https://www.favikon.com/blog/who-is-astro-alexandra). Pick one field, same format daily, lead with the fact that sounds false.

Depths of Wikipedia (adjacent curiosity model). Launched April 2020, first wave from a larger account's share July 2020, then ~1–2% growth per week to 500K by Feb 2022, 1M by July 2022, 1.5M+ by Feb 2026 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Depths_of_Wikipedia ; https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/growing-depthsofwikipedia-to-500-000-followers-wikipedia/id1436233955?i=1000551165073 ; https://michigantoday.umich.edu/2022/08/26/from-rabbit-hole-to-raging-success). Cheap static format, strict editorial voice, one seeding share.

Non-English static-card feeds. @ense_de_ciencia 2M with 41K posts; Brazilian creators sit above most English individual science creators (https://www.usepora.com.br/melhores-influenciadores-de-ciencia). Language-market ownership plus newsroom cadence.

Trial Reels: Instagram's own data says 40% of creators who try them post more Reels and 80% of those see higher non-follower reach (https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels ; https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-allows-creators-to-schedule-trial-reels/816549/).

## 3. Format breakdown: what generates shares and saves

Ranking signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; sends are the growth signal (https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/instagram-algorithm-2026 ; https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers). "DM share = 3–5x a like" is unofficial (https://socialday.live/features/instagrams-dm-share-signal-now-drives-3-to-5-times-more-reach-than-likes).

Carousels vs Reels. Socialinsider 2026 (25M posts): carousels ~0.50–0.55% engagement by followers, Reels 0.48%, images 0.33%; carousels drive the most saves; Reels remain the reach format (https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram ; https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagram-carousels-beat-reels-for-engagement-in-2026-and-when-to-use-each/ ; https://contentdrips.com/blog/2026/06/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-2026-guide/). Education is the top-engaging industry at 2.10% median ER, "driven by carousel infographics and tutorial Reels" (https://apaya.com/blog/social-media-benchmarks-instagram). Per-follower engagement down ~17% YoY (Rival IQ 0.36% to 0.30%, via https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-engagement-rate-benchmarks-2026/).

Science-specific evidence. December 2025 Computers in Human Behavior study: median video views 3x higher on TikTok than Reels and 25x for cross-posted content; Instagram average interaction rate 3.9%; engineering gets likes, social science gets comments; "snappy, objective" wins on TikTok, detailed slightly ironic wins on YouTube; emotional tone does not predict success (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0747563225003139 ; https://www.psypost.org/how-science-goes-viral-the-hidden-rules-of-tiktok-instagram-and-youtube/). Content made for TikTok and dumped on Instagram underperforms; Instagram rewards native framing and the carousel/save behaviour. 2023 Journalism and Media study on "information" vs "presentation" approaches (https://doi.org/10.3390/journalmedia4040071); 2020 PLOS One: interpersonal strategies (asking questions, replying) increase science conversations (https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0241972).

High-performing formats with examples: single-object physical demo, payoff in first second (https://www.instagram.com/physicsfun/?hl=en ; https://www.instagram.com/thephysicsgirl/reel/CzYz0CUq6Bk/); question-reveal-twist animation (https://www.instagram.com/zackdfilms/); dark-history cold open (https://www.instagram.com/sci_phile/reel/C1aE8hEP2A2/); kid-reaction experiment (https://www.instagram.com/sarveshtripathimaxscience/reel/DAh20IJM2bk/); event milestone content (https://www.instagram.com/nasaartemis/reels/); credited wildlife spectacle (https://www.instagram.com/nature/?hl=en); high-volume static cards for a language market (https://www.instagram.com/ense_de_ciencia/p/C02xUtYK6Ai/). Hook guidance: viewers decide in 1–2 seconds, skips before 3 seconds penalised, never open with "Hi I'm…", on-screen text and voiceover must promise the same thing (https://www.youcanknowthings.com/getting-started-on-instagram/ ; https://www.upgrow.com/blog/10-proven-instagram-reel-hooks-that-stop-the-scroll-in-under-3-seconds ; https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-hook-formulas).

## 4. Visual identity patterns

Grid and cover. Since January 2025 the grid crops every thumbnail to 3:4 (1080x1440); 4:5 loses a thin side strip, squares get padded, 9:16 covers are cut to 3:4; creators can set custom grid thumbnails (https://nealschaffer.com/instagram-post-size/ ; https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagrams-new-grid-layout-size-and-dimensions-2025/ ; https://almcorp.com/blog/instagram-thumbnail-editing-profile-grid/). Design at 4:5 with a centred safe zone or natively at 3:4. Top accounts use a 3–6 word cover title in the vertical middle third (observed, unverified).

Colour. Kurzgesagt: flat vector, saturated neon on deep navy; a documented palette is #001372, #0b0054, #e30050, #e32e01, #fbbe00 (https://www.color-hex.com/color-palette/81161 ; https://artsatmichigan.umich.edu/ink/2019/09/27/simple-bright-beautiful-the-work-of-kurzgesagt). Zack D Films: Pixar-like lit 3D with consistent characters. Institutions rely on the photograph and keep chrome minimal (https://www.warc.com/en/article/how-national-geographic-wins-on-instagram-d6d079a031614e0589d0961b5908d442). Creator convention: dark backgrounds with one accent for physics/space; off-white with black type for biology/health (observed, unverified).

Typography and captions. Burned-in captions for accessibility and sound-off hooks (https://stackinfluence.com/blog/best-instagram-hooks-for-content-creators ; https://www.youcanknowthings.com/getting-started-on-instagram/). Heavy geometric sans-serif, 3–5 words per caption line, keyword in accent colour, above the lower UI band; watermark small handle bottom-left or top-centre (observed, unverified).

Carousel conventions: headline-only first slide, one idea per slide, a figure or micrograph as anchor, final "save this" or source slide (https://www.visualizeyourscience.com/blog/carousels).

## 5. Gaps where a new account could win

Hindi/Indian-language explainer video on Instagram. Indian "science" ranking lists are thin (https://www.heepsy.com/top-instagram/science/india). Hindi science YouTubers have large YouTube audiences (GetSetFlySCIENCE 4M subs: https://www.thereelstars.com/entertainment/indias-science-influencers-sharing-knowledge-across-generations/) but small Instagram footprints (@gareebscientist 16K); unofficial Hindi dubs of Zack D Films exist. A bilingual English/Hindi Reel with burned-in captions in both scripts has no verified large incumbent.

Curated "wonder" clips with rigorous captions. @wonderofscience has 7,450 IG followers; the generic @science handle is run by a meme group. A curated clip account that credits sources and explains the mechanism is unoccupied at scale.

Paper-of-the-day. No Instagram account built around one peer-reviewed paper per day surfaced; closest models are text-only (Astrobites: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.09496) and @arxiv (https://www.instagram.com/arxiv/). Carousels drive saves and @sciencemagazine grew from 200K to 517K on research imagery, so a daily 6-slide "one paper, one figure, one takeaway" carousel with a Reel cut-down is a plausible wedge.

Quantum and condensed-matter depth. Largest quantum handles are 383K and 101K, both static aggregators; no verified animated quantum creator on Instagram at @quantumfracture's Spanish scale (877K).

Explainer depth between meme and lecture. 45–90 second mechanism-level Reels are underexploited compared with 15-second fact drops (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0747563225003139 ; https://news.mit.edu/2025/3-questions-hank-green-science-communication-and-curiosity-0529).

Event tentpoles. Pre-planned explainers for each 2026–27 launch, eclipse and prize cycle can ride the same demand institutions capture (https://www.nasa.gov/general/nasas-artemis-ii-breaks-agency-streaming-record/).

## 6. Anti-patterns that keep mid-tier science accounts stuck

Captions that do not teach ("9 out of 10 times it's because their captions are really basic": https://www.signalsblog.ca/how-to-use-instagram-to-communicate-real-science-really-well/). Lecturing rather than being a person (https://scifundchallenge.org/self-guided-class-instagram/instagram-class-part-6-examples-of-successful-scicomm-on-instagram/). Wasting the first three seconds; on-screen text that promises something different from the voiceover. Cross-posting TikTok cuts unchanged. Optimising for likes when sends per reach moves discovery. Square or landscape assets the 3:4 grid crops into unreadable tiles. Ignoring Trial Reels. Format drift: AsapSCIENCE (639K, flat) and Physics Girl (151K) stalled after leaving a single repeatable format; physicsfun's decade of one format reached 2M. Chasing "science" as a topic rather than a field: generic aggregators win on volume; the growth stories each own one field or one format. Trusting influencer-ranking lists.

## Immediate implications for @hqrs_1

Own one field and one repeatable format before widening. Reels for reach and carousels for saves in roughly a 3:2 weekly ratio, both 3:4-safe with a mid-frame text title. Lead every Reel with the false-sounding claim in the first second and burn in captions. Trial Reels on every new hook. Build a tentpole calendar. Consider a Hindi/English bilingual line and a daily paper carousel, the two gaps with the clearest evidence. Measure sends per reach and saves per reach, not likes.
