## 2. Recurring viral formats for science accounts in 2026

Platform context: raw, one-take content reported to outperform produced content this month; "first two seconds decide everything"; 7–15s cited as the completion sweet spot by SocialPilot/Later (https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-trends ; https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/), while Moonb claims 45–60s pulls the highest median views (https://www.moonb.io/blog/instagram-reel-length). These conflict: very short for hooks/demos, ~45–60s for explainers, test with Trial Reels. Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to non-followers (https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm). Ranking signals Mosseri has confirmed: watch time first, sends-per-reach for non-follower reach, saves above likes (https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-algorithm/ ; https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers). A Computers in Human Behavior study found emotional tone does not predict success for science content (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0747563225003139); a JCOM study found TikTok science influencers rarely credit sources (https://jcom.sissa.it/article/pubid/JCOM_2502_2026_A03/). Citing sources on screen is a differentiator.

Formats:
- Dated-hoax debunk. Hook: the hoax headline on screen, then "No." 20–40s talking head. Caption: verdict, mechanism, source.
- "Scientists can't explain it" honest-uncertainty explainer. Hook: the anomaly in one sentence. 30–60s; text-on-screen sigma numbers.
- Numbered body/physiology breakdown and scale comparison: the two science formats FlowShorts lists as reliable on Reels (https://flowshorts.app/instagram-reels-ideas/science). 15–30s; caption repeats the list for saves.
- "That's impossible" physics demo then resolution. Counterintuitive moment in frame 1; 15–30s (https://fluxnote.io/guides/viral-physics-youtube-shorts-ideas-2026 ; https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/physics-girl-why-her-science-videos-are-still-going-viral-in-2026/69299288 ; https://viralballs.com/en/blog/10-satisfying-ball-physics-video-ideas-that-go-viral).
- AI-generated speculative "what if" visualisations. Vendor claim of 4.2x comments (https://www.is4.ai/blog/our-blog-1/viral-ai-videos-2026-how-they-were-made-413 ; unverified). Sora shut down March 2026; Veo 3.1/Kling dominate (https://www.eweek.com/news/sora-alternatives-ai-video-tools-2026/). Label AI clearly.
- Expert stitch/duet debunk of a health claim (https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-00472-5).
- Casual "FaceTime-style" storytelling / day-in-the-lab (https://blog.mean.ceo/viral-trends-on-social-media-september-2026/ ; TikTok Discover List 2026: https://newsroom.tiktok.com/discover-list-2026-meet-the-creators-to-watch?lang=en).
- "Potential-Maxxing" numbers hook (https://buffer.com/resources/trending-audio-instagram/): "145°F. That's the new limit for complex life."
- Carousels for saves: reported 0.50% engagement rate, highest of any format; 6–10 slides optimal (https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-content-formats-guide/ ; https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/instagram-reach-2026-algorithm-reels-carousels-caption-seo).
- Text-on-screen hook rules (https://www.heyorca.com/blog/the-best-social-media-hooks-for-2026 ; https://www.ramd.am/blog/trends-instagram). Caption SEO repeatedly cited for search reach.
