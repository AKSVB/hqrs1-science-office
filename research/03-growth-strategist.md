Role: Growth Strategist. Filed 2026-09-27.

Method note. WebFetch was blocked for every domain by the proxy and the WebSearch budget ran out after ~200 queries, so every claim comes from search-result extracts. Labels: (IG-confirmed) when the source attributes it to Instagram/Meta/Mosseri directly; (measured) when it comes from a named dataset; (third-party, unverified) when it is a blog assertion; (assumption) for modelling inputs.

## 1. How ranking works in 2026

Structure. Instagram runs separate ranking systems for Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels; Instagram's own explainer lists the signals per surface (about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained; summaries at https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-algorithm/ and https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-algorithm/). Feed weighs your activity, post popularity, the poster, interaction history; Stories weigh viewing history, engagement history, closeness; Explore weighs post popularity, your Explore activity, history with the poster; Reels weigh your activity, history with the poster, information about the Reel (audio, visuals, popularity) and the poster.

The three signals Mosseri named (IG-confirmed, January 2025, reiterated through 2026): watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach (DM shares). Likes matter slightly more for connected reach (followers), sends slightly more for unconnected reach (non-followers). Sources: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/ ; https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/ ; https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers ; https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/instagram-algorithm-2026 . Instagram has not published numeric weights; "DM shares carry 3–5x the weight of likes" is a third-party estimate (https://socialday.live/features/instagrams-dm-share-signal-now-drives-3-to-5-times-more-reach-than-likes ; https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026/). Treat the ordering (watch time > sends > likes for discovery) as confirmed and multipliers as folklore.

Follower count is not a ranking input; signals are per post, which is why small accounts can out-reach large ones on Reels (https://wonderworksdesign.net/guide/instagram ; https://www.virallized.com/does-follower-count-matter-on-instagram). Mosseri tells creators to focus on reach rather than followers (https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-metrics-to-focus-creators-on-views/723645/). Third-party analyses report non-follower views rose from ~30% to 49% of all Instagram views between 2024 and end-2025, and >50% of Reel views typically come from non-followers (https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-trends-2026-creators-marketers/ ; https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-metrics/).

Views as the primary metric (IG-confirmed, August 2024): "Views" is the headline metric across all formats; impressions, plays and Story impressions deprecated through 2025 (https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-metrics-to-focus-creators-on-views/723645/ ; https://meetedgar.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-updates).

Original content over reposts (IG-confirmed). Meta's Q2 2025 call: over two-thirds of US Instagram recommendations were original content, up from 60% of Reels in Q3 2024 (https://www.threads.com/@mattnavarra/post/DM7vOI6KT0O ; https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2025/q2/META-Q2-2025-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf). Q4 2025 call (28 Jan 2026): original-content prevalence rose to 75% of recommendations; US Reels watch time up >30% YoY (https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/01/28/meta-meta-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/). July 2025: Instagram stopped recommending accounts that primarily repost Reels; 30 April 2026: extended to photos and carousels (https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator/ ; https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/ ; https://routenote.com/blog/instagram-tightens-rules-reposted-content/ ; https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/). Details: penalty is on recommendation surfaces only; watermark, credit tag, screenshot with credit, or speed change do not make content original; "meaningful edits" (own commentary, analysis, context, original graphics) can; rolling 30-day window. The "10 or more reposts in 30 days" threshold appears only in third-party guides (https://growvib.com/blog/instagram-aggregator-crackdown-repost-accounts-2026 ; https://www.viallo.app/blog/instagram-repost-rules-creators). "40–60% more reach for originals" claims are unverified (https://www.launchvibes.tech/articles/instagram-original-content-algorithm).

Watermarks. Instagram's ranking explainer says low-resolution, watermarked or visibly recycled Reels are less likely to be recommended. "50–70% suppression" figures are third-party (https://cut.pro/en/blog/instagram-buries-watermarked-reposts-2026).

Trial Reels (IG-confirmed feature). Shown only to non-followers, not on grid or in followers' feeds; after ~24 hours you see views, likes, comments and shares versus previous trials; manual share or auto-share if it performs within 72 hours (https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagram-trial-reels-work-72-hours/ ; https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels). Access reportedly for creator/business accounts with 1,000+ followers (https://www.socialchamp.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels/) (reported, not confirmed).

Reels length (IG-confirmed for 3 minutes). Mosseri, January 2025: Reels up to three minutes, still focused on short form (https://www.threads.com/@mosseri/post/DE-efFqyStv ; https://www.threads.com/@mosseri/post/DE-fuy2yFZv). Uploads of 15–20 minutes reportedly rolling out but Reels over three minutes not recommended to non-followers (https://www.moonb.io/blog/instagram-reel-length ; https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-length/) (third-party). Licensing limits on 3-minute audio (https://www.threads.com/@mosseri/post/DFitJNnSidg).

Other 2025–26 changes. Edits app launched 30 April 2025 with 2026 updates (https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-edits/ ; https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-previews-updates-for-its-edits-app/818024/). "Your Algorithm" for Reels launched 10 Dec 2025 in the US, letting viewers add/remove interest topics (https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/reels-algorithm-control ; https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/meta/instagram-hands-you-the-keys-to-control-your-algorithm-in-reels-plans-to-expand ; https://thenextweb.com/news/instagram-your-algorithm-central-experience-mosseri): topic clarity matters, a science account should be legible as "science". Grid moved to 3:4 previews (Jan 2025); grid reordering June 2025 (https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/12/instagram-will-finally-let-you-rearrange-your-grid). No evidence of a "Reels-only profile" format. New Reels Insights (April 2026): skip rate (scrolled past within ~3 seconds), share rate, views over time, post-watch behaviour (profile visit, follow) (https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-insights-update-2025 ; https://www.babbleboxx.com/post/instagram-adds-reels-retention-skip-rate-what-influencer-marketers-should-do-next ; https://almcorp.com/blog/instagram-insights-update-new-metrics/). AI: optional "AI creator" account label May 2026, no distribution effect stated (https://www.engadget.com/2162426/instagram-is-testing-optional-ai-creator-labels/ ; https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/414862/instagram-unveils-new-ai-creator-account-label.html). Hashtags: hashtag following removed Dec 2024; Mosseri: hashtags help topic discovery but "won't necessarily increase your content's visibility" (https://www.phable.io/phable-labs/instagram-hashtags-keywords-discovery ; https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-hashtags-relevant-2025). Reported cap of five hashtags unverified (https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/instagram-caps-hashtags-at-five-to-combat-spam).

## 2. What a sub-10k account should do

Cadence (measured). Buffer's 2M+ post analysis: 3–5 posts/week saw ~12% higher reach per post than lower-frequency accounts, rising to ~24% at 10+ posts/week; no fatigue penalty (https://buffer.com/resources/social-media-frequency-guide/ ; https://sociallyin.com/blog/can-you-post-too-much-on-instagram/ ; https://finance.yahoo.com/news/study-suggests-posting-more-often-162918616.html). Buffer benchmark average ~17 posts/month (https://buffer.com/resources/social-media-benchmarks/). Mosseri: a couple of feed posts per week plus daily Stories (https://www.serotonin.co.uk/news/what-adam-mosseris-high-performing-social-media-strategy-looks-like-in-2025); fewer than 4–5 Stories/day is ideal (https://www.threads.com/@oncescuradu/post/DJe-6ZfMDTW ; https://smk.co/instagram-chief-explains-stories-rankings/). Recommendation: 4–5 Reels/week (one or two as Trial Reels), 1–2 carousels/week, 2–4 Stories/day.

Formats (measured). Reels are the reach format: Socialinsider 2026 average Reel reach rate 30.81%, more than double carousels, images or Stories (https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram ; https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-metrics/). Carousels win on engagement per follower (carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.48%, images 0.33%). Carousel resurgence: https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/icymi-carousels-are-back-baby . Reels for discovery, carousels for saveable reference, static rarely.

Reel length (measured). Socialinsider's 6M-Reel study: 45–60 second band had the highest median views (10,374) and engagement rate (0.35%), roughly double clips under 30s or over 3 minutes (https://www.moonb.io/blog/instagram-reel-length ; https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/). Retention, not duration, ranks (https://retensis.com/blog/good-instagram-reels-retention-rate). Rule: as long as the payoff needs, 30–60s for most explainers; first 1.5–3 seconds must state the question or show the phenomenon.

Hooks and captions (measured). Socialinsider's 9.1M-post study: captions under 30 words have the highest engagement; Instagram truncates at ~125 characters (https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-caption-length/ ; https://blog.hootsuite.com/ideal-social-media-post-length/). Short caption restating the question and inviting a specific reply.

Hashtags and keywords. Instagram indexes captions, alt text, bio, on-screen text (https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/instagram-seo ; https://rankite.com/blog/instagram-seo-tips). Use 3–5 topical hashtags, key term in first line, alt text on every slide, say the topic aloud early. "30% more reach" claim unverified (https://lamplightcreatives.com/captions-vs-hashtags-instagram-2026/).

Trial Reels. Run every risky format as a Trial; enable auto-share (https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagram-trial-reels-work-72-hours/).

Collabs and remixes. Collab posts pool engagement; can be added after publishing (https://metricool.com/instagram-news/ ; https://www.instagram.com/mosseri/reel/DHGTfLkSGbb/). Collab weekly with 1k–20k science/education accounts. Remixes inherit some distribution (https://smmcompare.com/blog/instagram-reels-remix-collab-features-growth-2026); use them to add explanation to a viral clip, which also satisfies the "meaningful edit" test.

Cross-posting. Socialinsider's 69M-video study (Jan 2025–Jul 2026): YouTube Shorts strongest reach at every size; for the smallest accounts TikTok trails Reels (https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-vs-reels-vs-shorts/). Post natively to all three from the clean master (https://www.socialync.io/blog/avoid-content-duplication-penalties-cross-posting-2026). Never upload a TikTok-watermarked file.

Comments, DMs, Stories, channels, Notes. Reply to every comment in the first hour; a March 2026 "conversation signal" is plausible-unconfirmed (https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers). 2–4 Stories/day with poll or question sticker; reshare each Reel to Stories. Broadcast-channel "20–40% higher Story views" claims are vendor assertions (https://www.sendible.com/insights/instagram-broadcast-channels). No 2026 data on Notes.

## 3. Benchmarks (science/education, 2025–26)

Engagement rate. Socialinsider 2026 (25M posts): average by followers 0.48% (down 24% YoY); Quid 2026 median 0.30%; engagement by reach fell from 3.70% (Jan 2025) to 3.20% (Aug 2026) (https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram ; https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/ ; https://apaya.com/blog/social-media-benchmarks-instagram). Rival IQ 2025: median 0.36%; Higher Education 2.10%, top industry (https://www.rivaliq.com/benchmark-reports/ ; https://colorlib.com/wp/instagram-engagement-rate/). HypeAuditor 2026: nano creators (1k–10k) average 1.78% by followers (https://www.amraandelma.com/instagram-influencer-engagement-rate-statistics/ ; https://nowadays.media/blog/influencer-engagement-rate-benchmarks-2026-by-platform-niche-follower-tier/); other compilations put nano at 2.53% (https://www.upgrow.com/blog/instagram-engagement-rate-statistics-2026-industry-follower-size). Working target: 2–3% by followers on Reels, 4–6% by reach.

Reach rate. Reels ~30.81% of followers on average. A healthy Reel for a sub-10k account shows views well above follower count.

Follower growth. ~22%/year for 1k–5k accounts, ~11% above 100k (https://nowadays.media/blog/influencer-engagement-rate-benchmarks-2026-by-platform-niche-follower-tier/) (third-party). Growth slowing across sizes (https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/).

Follower conversion. No published benchmark for follows per 1,000 non-follower views (https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-analytics-creators-metrics-guide/ ; https://www.virallized.com/instagram-metrics-to-track). One self-reported log: ~1,100 views per Reel and ~40 new followers/week early, rising to ~4,800 views/Reel and 11,000 followers by month six (https://www.lilachbullock.com/grow-instagram-followers-views-organically/); implies ~9 follows per 1,000 views. One anecdote. April 2026 post-watch metrics show follows per Reel directly; measure own rate within two weeks.

## 4. Growth math

Model: new followers = non-follower views x follow rate. Inputs (assumptions): non-follower share 50–70%; follow rate per 1,000 non-follower views: pessimistic 3, base 9, optimistic 20.

Required non-follower views: +1,000 followers needs 333k / 111k / 50k; +10,000 needs 3.3M / 1.1M / 500k; +100,000 needs 33M / 11M / 5M.

Weekly, at base rate 9/1,000 and 60% non-follower share: +1,000 in 12 weeks needs ~15k total views/week (~3,000 per Reel at 5/week); +10,000 in 26 weeks needs ~71k/week (~14k per Reel); +100,000 in 52 weeks needs ~355k/week (~70k per Reel). Pessimistic triples these; optimistic more than halves them. Reading: 1k is achievable on consistent 3–5k-view Reels; 10k needs a repeatable 10–20k-view format or a few 100k+ outliers; 100k needs several 1M+ Reels or a sustained 50k+ average. Re-run monthly with the observed follow rate.

## 5. What gets accounts throttled

(a) Reposting/aggregating: removed from recommendations if most posts in a rolling 30 days are unoriginal. (b) Watermarked or visibly recycled Reels. (c) Reels over three minutes (third-party). (d) Engagement bait ("tag 3 friends", "like for A") limits reach (https://www.socialync.io/blog/how-to-avoid-spam-fraudulent-uploading-2026 ; https://www.kofluence.com/blog/is-instagram-shadowban-real-or-myth/). (e) Follow/unfollow, mass following, automation. (f) AI label itself does not affect distribution, but low-effort recycled content is what the originality system targets. (g) Banned/spammy hashtags (https://hashtagtools.io/blog/banned-instagram-hashtags-shadowban-check-2026). (h) Low-resolution, bordered or text-heavy video. Check Settings > Account Status.

## 6. Weekly analytics

Track per post and weekly: views, non-follower share, average watch time and retention curve, skip rate, share rate (sends/reach), saves/reach, likes/reach, comments, profile visits, follows from post (https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-insights-update-2025 ; https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-metrics/). Double down when a format's median Reel beats the trailing-8-week median on views by 2x and sits in the top quartile on share rate and follows per 1,000 views. Kill or rework after 4–6 posts if skip rate is in the worst quartile, average watch time under a third of length, share rate below median, follows/1,000 under half base rate. External comparators: engagement by reach ~3.2%, Reels reach rate ~31%.

## 7. Monetisation path

Gifts on Reels: reported at 500 followers with a professional account (https://follownow.io/blog/instagram-followers-to-monetize) (third-party). Subscriptions: 10,000+ followers, 18+, professional account, eligible country (https://help.instagram.com/478012211024479 ; https://influencermarketinghub.com/instagram-subscriptions-gifts/). Reels bonuses: invite-only, seasonal (https://fluxnote.io/guides/instagram-reels-bonus-eligibility-requirements-2026 ; https://predis.ai/resources/get-invited-to-instagram-reels-bonus/). Brand deals and affiliate need no threshold; brands shifting to nano creators (https://www.emarketer.com/content/smaller-creators-deliver-efficiency-roi-pressure-mounts). Sequence: affiliate and brand deals from ~5–10k, subscriptions and Patreon at 10k+, bonuses if invited, courses once a newsletter list exists.

Bottom line: 4–5 original Reels per week (30–60s, question-first hook, no watermarks, science term in first line and alt text), 1–2 saveable carousels, 2–4 Stories daily, every risky idea as a Trial Reel, one collab per week, cross-post natively to Shorts and TikTok, reply to all comments in hour one, judge on watch time, share rate and follows per 1,000 views.
