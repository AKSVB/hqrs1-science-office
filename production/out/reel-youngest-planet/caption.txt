Earth is 4.5 billion years old. This planet is not even 1 million.
Astrophysical Journal Letters, 16 Sept 2026. Bernardi, Cieza and colleagues, Universidad Diego Portales.

Elias 2-24 b is the youngest planet ever found: under 1 million years old, roughly Jupiter's mass (models give about 2 to 4), about 450 light-years away and still embedded in its disk, about 55 au from its star. Found in archival Keck data by a Universidad Diego Portales team led by Andrea Bernardi. Astrophysical Journal Letters, 16 Sept 2026.
