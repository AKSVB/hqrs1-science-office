An AI, quote, solved, unquote, a million dollar maths problem. That is the headline. Here is the contested version. OpenAI says its agents proved that a forced Navier-Stokes fluid can blow up. On September eighth, it announced that around ten thousand agents, running for about eighty eight hours, produced that proof, with a further seventeen hours to formalise it in Lean. Forced versus unforced matters here, and OpenAI itself says it will not claim the prize. Three days later, twenty five Fields Medallists published a statement titled A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics. The work is not peer-reviewed, and a priority dispute has been reported. Big claim, big pushback.
