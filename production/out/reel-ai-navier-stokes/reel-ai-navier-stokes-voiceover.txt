An AI, quote, solved, unquote, a million dollar maths problem. That is the headline. Here is the contested version. On September eighth, OpenAI announced that a system of around ten thousand agents, running for about eighty eight hours, produced a proof addressing forced versions of the Navier-Stokes equations, with a further seventeen hours to formalise it in Lean. Forced versus unforced matters here, and OpenAI itself says it will not claim the prize. Three days later, twenty five Fields Medalists published a statement calling AI benchmark mathematics a severe misalignment. The work is not peer-reviewed, and a priority dispute has been reported. Big claim, big pushback. We will follow it. Sources in the caption.
