Your body rebuilds itself faster than you think.

Sender & Milo (Nature Medicine, 2021) modelled cell turnover across every tissue. Result: roughly 330 billion cells replaced per day, about 1% of all your cells. Blood cells dominate by count (~86%), gut lining by mass share is next.

The 'every 7 years' number is a folk myth; there is no single turnover time. Neurons in the cortex mostly last a lifetime. Gut epithelium turns over in days.
