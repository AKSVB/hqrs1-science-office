While you watched this, your body replaced about 90 million cells.

Sender & Milo modelled cell turnover across every tissue (Nature Medicine, 2021): ~330 billion cells a day, ~1% of you. Most of that is blood. The '7 years' claim is folklore; cortical neurons mostly last a lifetime.

Source: Sender R, Milo R. Nat Med 27, 45-48 (2021).
