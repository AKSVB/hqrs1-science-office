While you watch this, your body is replacing about four million cells every second. Three hundred and thirty billion a day. About one percent of you. Most of it is blood: red cells and neutrophils live short lives in huge numbers. You've probably heard that every cell is replaced every seven years. That's not true. Gut lining lasts days. Fat cells, about a decade. And most of the neurons in your cortex are as old as you are. So the body you had yesterday is mostly gone. The mind that remembers it is not.
