Paralysed and unable to speak. Their avatar talks and waves, straight from their brain. This is a brain-computer interface from the Chang lab at UCSF. A high-density implant called ECoG records signals from the brain. Machine-learning decoders turn those signals into speech and into gestures, like nodding and waving, and an on-screen avatar performs both at the same time. Now the honest part. Three participants, two of them driving the avatar in real time, with a restricted vocabulary, over a wired connection, in a lab. This is a demonstration, not a treatment you can go and get.
