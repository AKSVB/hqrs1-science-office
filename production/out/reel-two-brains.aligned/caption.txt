Two lineages build your brain, and they never mix.
Nature Neuroscience, 18 Sept 2026. Dundes, Jokhai, Loh and colleagues, Stanford Medicine.

Two cell lineages, Otx2 and Gbx2, build the brain from gastrulation onward and never mix, traced in mouse embryos. The same split appears in chick, zebrafish and acorn worms, lineages that diverged around 550 million years ago. Loh lab, Stanford Medicine. Nature Neuroscience, 18 Sept 2026. 'Two organs' is press framing; the paper shows two lineages.
