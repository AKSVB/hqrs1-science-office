A brain organoid kept alive for five years matured on a schedule: fetal-like, newborn-like, then early-childhood-like gene expression, with DNA methylation clocks matching time in the dish. About 425,000 cells profiled from about 110 organoids. Resemblance is molecular, not functional. Which childhood-onset disorder would you study with it first? Faravelli, Arlotta and colleagues, Nature, 19 Aug 2026.
