Two forager skeletons from Sulawesi show a novel tooth-wear pattern and traces of arecoline, from betel nut, today the fourth most used psychoactive substance. Griffith University (Adam Brumm). Science Advances, 9 Sept 2026. Caveats: n = 2; the older skeleton is dated 16,000 to 25,000 years, and headlines quote the top. The authors say it may be the earliest evidence of drug use.
