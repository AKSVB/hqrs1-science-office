Humans were getting high twenty five thousand years ago. That is the headline. Here is what is actually behind it. Two forager skeletons from Sulawesi, in Indonesia. Two. That is the whole sample. Their teeth show a wear pattern nobody had described before, plus chemical traces of arecoline, which comes from betel nut, today the world's fourth most common addictive drug. Now the date. It is a range, somewhere between sixteen thousand and twenty five thousand years. The headlines quote the top of that range. Even at the bottom, it is the earliest evidence of human drug use found so far. Science Advances, this month. Source in the caption.
