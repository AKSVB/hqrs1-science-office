The headline says humans got high twenty five thousand years ago. Here is what is actually behind it. Two forager skeletons from Sulawesi, in Indonesia. Two. That is the whole sample. Their teeth show a wear pattern nobody had described before, plus chemical traces of arecoline, which comes from betel nut, today the world's fourth most used psychoactive substance, a mild stimulant. Now the date. The older skeleton is dated somewhere between sixteen thousand and twenty five thousand years. The headlines quote the top of that range. Even so, the authors say it may be the earliest evidence of drug use found so far.
