Something is reproducing at 145 F. And it is not a bacterium.
Cell, 22 Sept 2026. Rappaport, Oliverio and colleagues, Syracuse University.

Incendiamoeba cascadensis, the 'fire amoeba', divides at 145 F (63 C) and survived a five-minute spike to 158 F (70 C) in a protective state. The previous limit for complex (eukaryotic) life was about 140 F. Found in Lassen Volcanic National Park. Rappaport, Oliverio and colleagues, Syracuse University. Cell, 22 Sept 2026.
