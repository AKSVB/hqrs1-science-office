Something is reproducing at one hundred and forty five degrees Fahrenheit. And it is not a bacterium. It is an amoeba, a complex cell like the ones in your body, and it has just set the heat record for complex life. Meet Incendiamoeba cascadensis, the fire amoeba. It reproduces at one hundred and forty five degrees and survives up to one hundred and fifty eight. Complex cells were thought to have a lower limit than that. The study is Syracuse led and appears in the journal Cell this month. One single cell, one very hot record. The limits of life keep moving. Source in the caption.
