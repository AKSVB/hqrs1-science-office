13 atoms simulated particles snapping apart.
Nature Physics, 23 Sept 2026. De, Monroe; Duke Quantum Center.

Thirteen ytterbium ions simulated string breaking, an analogue of confined particles snapping into new pairs (quarks are the analogy, not the experiment). The pairs formed at the ends and spread inward. A lattice-model simulation, not real quarks. Which idea should atoms act out next?
