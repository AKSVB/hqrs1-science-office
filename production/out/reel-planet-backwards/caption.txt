This planet orbits its star backwards.
Astronomy & Astrophysics, Sept 2026; arXiv 2609.24870. Carteret, University of Geneva; NIRPS, La Silla.

GJ 3090 b, 73 light-years away, orbits its red dwarf tilted about 136 degrees: past sideways, into backwards. 2.2 Earth radii, 4.5 Earth masses, a 2.9-day year. First retrograde planet found around a red dwarf; no known companion. What flipped it?
