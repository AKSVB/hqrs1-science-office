GJ 3090 b, 73 light-years away, orbits its red dwarf backwards and tilted: obliquity about 136 degrees. 2.2 Earth radii, 4.5 Earth masses, a 2.9-day year. The first retrograde planet found around a red dwarf. Carteret et al., University of Geneva, NIRPS at La Silla. Astronomy & Astrophysics, September 2026; arXiv 2609.24870. What flipped it?
