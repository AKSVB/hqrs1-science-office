Sound does not fade. It jumps.
Science 393, 1217-1220, 17 Sept 2026, DOI 10.1126/science.aeh7535. Makihara, Szakiel, Safavi-Naeini and colleagues, Stanford.

Stanford physicists (Safavi-Naeini lab) directly watched single phonons, the quantum packets of vibration, jump between energy levels in a mechanical resonator in real time. Quantum jumps: ions 1986, photons 2007, sound 2026. Science, 17 Sept 2026, DOI 10.1126/science.aeh7535.
