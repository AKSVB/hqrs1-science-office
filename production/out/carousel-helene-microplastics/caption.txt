Hurricane Helene (September 2024) raised microplastic deposition to 3 to 22 times pre-storm levels at three mountain sites in western North Carolina. At Highlands Biological Station one storm delivered 18.8% of the year's microplastics, nearly one fifth; 4.2% and 4.6% at the other two sites. Austin Gray, Virginia Tech, with Western Carolina University and UNC. Integrated Environmental Assessment and Management, 25 September 2026. Where does mountain plastic come from?
