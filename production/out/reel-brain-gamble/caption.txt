Your gamble, readable 0.5 s early.
Nature Neuroscience, 15 Sept 2026. Starkweather, Chang, Knight; UCSF and UC Berkeley.

Six patients implanted for surgical monitoring played a video game with bombs and treasure chests. Two patches of orbitofrontal cortex about 2 cm apart did opposite jobs and together predicted the choice about 0.5 s before it was made. No free-will claim. Would you want to see your own signal?
