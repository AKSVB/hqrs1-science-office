Scotty, the biggest T. rex ever found, died with a rib fracture that never finished healing. Neutron and X-ray imaging at Oak Ridge National Laboratory revealed a mineralised blood-vessel network inside the wound, 66 million years on. Reported by ORNL and Smithsonian Magazine, September 2026; journal not yet verified. Saying the rib killed Scotty is a stretch.
