Elias 2-24 b is the youngest planet ever found: under 1 million years old, still inside the disk it formed from, about 450 light-years away and about 55 au from its star. Roughly Jupiter's mass; models give about 2 to 4. Found in archival Keck data from 2018 and 2020 by Andrea Bernardi's team at Universidad Diego Portales. What would you look for in old telescope archives? Astrophysical Journal Letters, 16 Sept 2026.
