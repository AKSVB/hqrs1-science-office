Dark matter may have just hit a detector. Once. But this is not a discovery. One event, a nuclear recoil at two hundred and forty eight kilo electron volts, over two hundred and twenty live days and a two point eight four tonne-year exposure. Known backgrounds produce an event like this about half a percent of the time. That is not the chance dark matter is real. It is the chance ordinary physics did this anyway. In sigma: three point four local, two point six global. Far short of a discovery. If it were a WIMP, its mass would be above two hundred G E V. The paper is a preprint, not yet peer-reviewed.
