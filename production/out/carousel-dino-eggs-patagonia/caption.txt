More than 250 titanosaur eggshell fragments have turned up in southernmost Patagonia, evidence of giant dinosaurs nesting at high latitude. The likely trick: mound nests heated by rotting plant matter, like compost. Coverage from 8 September 2026; the journal is not yet verified by our desk.
