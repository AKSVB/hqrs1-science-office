Nobody has opened this letter in four thousand years. Researchers just read it anyway. The letters come from Kültepe, the ancient city of Kanesh, in Anatolia. They are cuneiform tablets, still sealed inside clay envelopes that have never been broken. Instead of cracking them open, the team used a portable CT scanner. It sees through the clay, picks out the tablet inside, and the signs can be read from the scan. One of the letters is from a woman named Anna-anna, written to her merchant husband. Her words were sealed four thousand years ago and read without a single crack. The study is in npj Heritage Science. Source in the caption.
