Eight sanidine samples from the AD 79 Vesuvius eruption, collected at Oplontis, were dated with a recalibrated argon-argon method: 1,938 plus or minus 13 years, against 1,946 years from Pliny the Younger's eyewitness account. Precision 0.7%, accuracy 0.4%. The exact day of the eruption is debated; the year is not. Paul Renne, Berkeley Geochronology Center, UC Berkeley, University of Padua.
