Two charges sit at the end sites, drawn as amber discs of radius 20 with the amber glow; between them a "string": an amber line 8 px, round caps, drawn along the row at 40 percent opacity with a soft glow. A pair is drawn as two small discs of radius 12 at neighbouring sites, one amber and one cyan (a particle and its partner; the colours are schematic and are not labelled as charges), joined by a short amber string of their own. ### NEW SCENE SPEC: `ion-chain` (three.js)

- Name and options: `ion-chain`, `--var view=chain` (default) | `single` | `trap`; `--var n=13` (number of ions, 0 allowed).
- What is drawn: a linear ion trap reduced to its honest shape: two long gold electrode rails (BoxGeometry 0.06 by 0.06 by 12, MeshStandardMaterial metalness 1, roughness 0.25, colour #d9b24a) running parallel, 0.9 apart, from the near foreground into the dark; below them a flat dark chip surface (a plane, #121a30, roughness 0.6) with a faint grid of thinner grey electrode segments (instanced boxes every 0.5 units, dim) so it reads as a fabricated surface; above the rails, a very faint violet-blue laser sheet (a translucent plane, additive, opacity 0.06, with a soft `fbm` shimmer) crossing the chain at a shallow angle. The ions: `n` small spheres (radius 0.035) on the trap axis, 0.25 apart, centred, each with an emissive cool blue-white core (#dff6ff) and an additive sprite halo (radial gradient, radius 0.2, cyan-white, opacity 0.55). The ions and their halos are the cool light; the one warm source is a single amber point light at the upper left that puts a warm reflection along the top edges of both rails and nowhere else. No labels, no text, no cryostat, no chamber walls: black beyond the rails.
  - `chain`: the camera 25 degrees above the axis and 15 degrees to the side, the chain in the upper middle of the frame, both rails converging toward the upper right, the lower third black.
  - `single`: the camera 4 times closer on the seventh ion, its halo filling a quarter of the frame width, neighbours soft; depth of field simulated by blurring a far pass 22 px in 2D.
  - `trap`: the camera pulled back and higher, so the chip surface with its electrode grid fills the upper half and the chain is small at the centre; the rails run the full frame width.
- Lighting: the amber key (intensity 1.8, upper left, low elevation so it rakes along the rails), the ions' own emissive light (each ion is also a weak point light, intensity 0.15, cyan) lighting the rails and chip beneath the chain, hemisphere 0.05. Lower third under 12 percent luminance.
- Camera: 50 mm equivalent for `chain` and `trap`, 120 mm for `single`; subject at 0.40 of frame height.
- What varies with t: each ion's halo breathes plus or minus 12 percent at its own seeded phase (0.6 to 1.1 Hz), each ion jitters plus or minus 1.5 px (seeded sinusoids, the look of micromotion, not a claim), the laser sheet's shimmer drifts, and a 3 percent slow dolly over 3 s. Nothing else moves; ions never change count within a render (`n` is fixed per render).
- Subject-only alpha layer: the ions with their halos (the seventh ion and its two neighbours in `single`); no rails, no chip, no sheet.
- Budget: under 40k triangles; about 3 s per frame.
