## Note to the Graphics desk: grading

All 26 stills go through one grade before they enter a scene, so the feed reads as a single account and the code-drawn plates sit naturally on top. Lift the black point to #0a1224 (never pure black), pull the shadows toward the blue-cyan field (a small blue shift in the shadows, about plus 6 on the blue channel, minus 3 on red), keep midtones neutral, and let exactly one warm source per frame stay warm (the cell's glow, the planet's heat, the lamp on the chip); any second warm source is desaturated toward neutral in the grade or the still is regenerated. Cap global saturation so that nothing but that one warm source is more saturated than the cyan accent (#4fe3f0); skies, water and metal should sit in the #0d1426 to #22304f band. Add a 6 percent edge vignette and 2 percent fine monochrome grain so the stills and the flat plates share the same texture. Confirm the lower third of every still averages under 12 percent luminance before the scrim is applied, because the caption band lives there. Make the grade a single reusable ffmpeg filter chain (curves, colorbalance, eq for saturation, vignette, noise) stored beside the stills as `grade.sh`, apply it identically to every still, and keep the ungraded originals. Space stills (YP-01 to YP-06) are the exception case: the grade may not lift the black of deep space above #0a1224, and the Earth's daylight side is allowed to stay white-blue since it is the one cool highlight in that Reel.
